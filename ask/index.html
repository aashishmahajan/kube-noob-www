Bring your own AI

Your Claude, kube-noob's tools, in your browser

kube-noob has no AI inside; this page lets you bring yours. You paste your own Anthropic API key, Claude does the talking, and whenever it needs a fact it calls kube-noob's tools, which run here in this tab. This site has no backend: it never receives your key or your questions. They go from your browser straight to api.anthropic.com, billed to your key.

Before you paste a key: create a separate key for this page, in a workspace with a monthly spend limit, and never reuse a key that production systems or teammates depend on. Revoke it in the Claude Console when you are done. The key is kept in memory for this page, or in this tab's session storage only if you tick the box (cleared when the tab closes); it is never written to local storage, cookies or logs.
