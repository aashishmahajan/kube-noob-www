Licensing

Free and open source, today

kube-noob is released under the Apache License 2.0. You can use it, change it and run it, at work or at home, at no cost.

What may come

We are working out how kube-noob will be offered to companies that want more than the open-source releases, such as support, longer maintenance or features for larger teams. That may eventually mean paid tiers. Nothing is decided yet, and it will be announced here first. Releases already published stay under Apache-2.0: that license cannot be taken back.

Students: free

Student licenses are free. Register with your email and we send a license ID to that address; we keep it with your name and school, so if paid tiers ever arrive, your student access stays free.

Companies

Running kube-noob at work and want to talk about support or plans? Contact us and choose "Companies and licensing".

What you send goes to the kube-noob maintainer through a Google Apps Script web app and is kept in a private Google Sheet, only to reply to you or to keep your license. It is not sold or shared, and you can ask for it to be deleted through the contact form. More: privacy.
