Audit rules / KN-N002

KN-N002 Route without TLS

high · audit / network

What it finds

Plain-HTTP Routes expose credentials and session cookies to anyone on the path.

How to fix it

Add spec.tls with termination: edge (or reencrypt/passthrough) and insecureEdgeTerminationPolicy: Redirect.

NIST SP 800-53 controls

  • SC-8 Transmission Confidentiality and Integrity
  • SC-8(1) Transmission Confidentiality and Integrity | Cryptographic Protection

Check your YAML

Paste it in the playground (it runs in your browser), or run kube-noob audit -f deployment.yaml; kube-noob explain KN-N002 prints this rule. Rule ids never change meaning. All 77 rules.
